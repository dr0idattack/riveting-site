About the game Privacy policy

Questions

Can I play without buying anything?

Yes. Act I is free to play, start to finish. One purchase unlocks the full game. There are no ads and no item store.

I bought the game. How do I get it back?

In the game, open Settings > Purchases > Restore purchases. You need the Apple Account that made the purchase. On a new phone, sign that account into the App Store first.

Where are my saves?

On your device. Riveting has no accounts and no server. If you delete the app, your saves go with it. You cannot move saves between phones.

Which devices does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later. It also supports foldable iPhone screens, open and closed.

I found a bug.

Email me with the phone model, iOS version and a short description. If a run gave a wrong result, mention the hero, the act and the fight number. Seeded runs make bugs repeatable.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem or reportaproblem.apple.com to ask Apple for a refund.
