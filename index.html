Riveting

A steampunk roguelike pinball dungeon crawl for iPhone.

Aim a molten brass core into a board full of rivets. Every hit powers your hero's attack. Push through three dungeon acts, pick one of six heroes, and try to stay alive one more board.

Support Privacy policy

What makes a run fun

  • Every shot matters: lights, sound and haptics report each hit.
  • Every landed core drops into a bottom bucket and pays.
  • Land all three buckets to spin the Jackpot Engine.
  • Raised side ramps carry the core over the rivet field before it drops back in.
  • Three pinball layouts — Twin Foundry, Crossfire and Aether Coil — rotate through every run.

Replay it your way

  • Six heroes, each with its own attack rules and rank milestones.
  • Seeded runs, so a good run can be shared and replayed.
  • A full run crosses three dungeons in about 25 to 35 minutes.
  • Thirteen hand-painted story panels carry the campaign.
