Riveting Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone game Riveting by J.P. Thompson.

What we collect

Nothing. Riveting collects no data, sends no data and tracks nobody. The App Store product page lists our data practices as Data Not Collected.

How the app works without servers

Riveting has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads. Your game saves, settings and progress stay on your device. We never see them.

Purchases

Riveting sells one purchase that unlocks the full game. Apple processes the payment. We do not see your name, your payment details or your purchase records. Apple keeps purchase history in your Apple Account; you can manage it there or restore purchases from the game.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. It contains no ads, no social features and no in-app messaging.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete. To clear data from your device, delete the app. To manage your Apple purchases, use your Apple Account settings.

Useful Apple pages: Apple Privacy Policy and Apple's standard end user license agreement, which covers the app's license terms.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Riveting adds connected features that collect data, this page will name the data, the purpose and how to remove consent before that version ships. The date at the top changes with every edit.

Write to jpthomp137@gmail.com with any privacy question.
